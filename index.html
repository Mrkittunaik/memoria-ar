How it works

Three steps to a living memory

No coding, no special hardware. Just a photo, a video, and any camera.

01

Upload your photo + video

Upload the photo you've already printed — or plan to print — and the video you want to attach to it. We generate the AR target automatically.

02

Print and display the photo

Print it normally — any printer, any size, any paper. Put it in an album, frame it, stick it in a card. The AR link is embedded in the image itself.

03

Scan to watch the memory

Anyone with a phone can open the scanner link, point at the photo, and the video plays live inside the frame — perfectly tracked, no app download needed.

04

Relive it forever

Every time someone scans your photo, you see it in your dashboard. No expiry, no link to share — the photo itself is the trigger.

Features

Built for the magic moment

Everything needed to make the experience feel effortless.

Feature-based image tracking

Recognises photos at angles, in low light, partially covered, or rotated — not just head-on pixel matching.

Video anchored in real-time

As you move the phone, the video stays locked perfectly onto the photo — tracking scale, rotation, tilt, and perspective.

Mobile-first scanner

Opens camera in fullscreen instantly. No loading screens, no UI clutter. Just point and watch.

Scan analytics

See when, how often, and from where each memory is being viewed. Track which photos resonate most.

No app to install

Runs entirely in the browser using WebXR and MindAR. Recipients just open a link on any modern phone.

Private memories

Lock memories with a PIN so only intended recipients can view them. Perfect for personal gifts.
