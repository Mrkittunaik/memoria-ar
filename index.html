How it works

Three steps to a living memory

No coding, no special hardware. Just a photo, a video, and any camera.

01

Upload your photo + video

Upload the printed photo and the video you want to link. We generate the AR target automatically.

02

Print and display the photo

Any printer, any size, any paper. Frame it, put it in an album, stick it in a card.

03

Scan to watch the memory

Open the link, point at the photo — the video plays live inside the frame. No app needed.

04

Relive it forever

Every scan shows up in your dashboard. No expiry — the photo itself is the trigger.

Features

Built for the magic moment

Everything needed to make the experience feel effortless.

Feature-based image tracking

Recognises photos at angles, in low light, partially covered, or rotated.

Video anchored in real-time

The video stays locked onto the photo as you move — tracking rotation, tilt, and perspective.

Mobile-first scanner

Opens camera in fullscreen instantly. No loading screens, no UI clutter. Just point and watch.

Scan analytics

See when, how often, and from where each memory is being viewed.

No app to install

Runs entirely in the browser. Recipients just open a link on any modern phone.

Private memories

Lock memories with a PIN so only intended recipients can view them.
